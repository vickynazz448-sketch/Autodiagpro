AutoDiagPro is an independent automotive diagnostic information platform created to help technicians, workshop teams, students, and vehicle owners understand diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs), symptoms, electrical checks, live-data clues, and systematic troubleshooting. The platform promotes evidence-based testing rather than replacing parts based on a code alone. About AutoDiagPro

AutoDiagPro is an independent automotive diagnostic guide created by Asif Hossain, with supporting role credited to Zareen Ahmed. It is designed to make automotive troubleshooting easier to understand through structured diagnostic information, DTC references, electrical testing guidance, and symptom-led checks.

The platform encourages technicians to confirm faults using wiring information, manufacturer procedures, scan-tool data, voltage/ground checks, and appropriate component tests. A diagnostic code is a clue, not automatic proof that a named part has failed.

What the platform offers

  • General DTC explanations and possible fault areas.
  • Educational diagnostic workflows and electrical testing concepts.
  • General information about vehicle systems and modules.
  • Optional urgent diagnostic assistance, subject to availability and the terms shown before purchase.

Diagnostic Disclaimer & Safety

Important: A DTC is a starting point for diagnosis, not proof that a specific part has failed.
  • Verify code definitions and test procedures against the exact vehicle, engine, model year, wiring diagram, and manufacturer service information.
  • Use appropriate meters, scan tools, PPE, and safe lifting/support equipment. Disconnect power only where the correct service procedure requires it.
  • Never probe, energise, bypass, or dismantle airbag/SRS, high-voltage EV/hybrid, fuel, braking, or steering systems unless trained and following the correct procedure.
  • Do not drive a vehicle if a fault makes it unsafe. Seek a qualified technician or roadside/emergency service where appropriate.
  • Any values or examples shown are general guidance and may not match every vehicle or operating condition.
